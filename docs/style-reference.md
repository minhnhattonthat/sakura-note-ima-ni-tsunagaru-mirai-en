# Style reference

Voices per character (from the phase 6 survey of the whole script) and example pairs. Every translator matches
these; a line any speaker could have said is a mistake.

## General voice
- Audience: children and families. Easy, warm, funny English; contractions; short sentences. American spelling.
- The boy's inner thoughts are often framed in the third person (the narrator turns out to be the boy grown up,
  2527:79): keep that framing until the switch.
- Animals: what humans hear is barking/meowing; what the player reads is the animal's real speech. Keep both.
- Running jokes (Nazkaiser hero poses, Ginger Pork's food puns, Nobiyo's mangled lectures) must still be jokes.
- Nobiyo's tag and address: Nobiyo-sensei (honorifics kept, D1).
