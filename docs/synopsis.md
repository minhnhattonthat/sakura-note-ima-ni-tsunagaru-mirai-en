# Synopsis

Drafted from a read of the whole extracted script (phase 6 survey, codex). The script decides wording; this file decides who is who and what must not be revealed early.

The story follows a ten-year-old fifth-grader through a supernatural crisis during a three-day holiday. The boy, his family's cat, and Nanami's dog are complementary playable viewpoints, not mutually exclusive versions of the protagonist. Four chapters contain their linked episodes, with Tears unlocking additional episodes; four Kaname bonus episodes extend the family story. The reports do not establish every chapter boundary precisely, so the ranges below identify scenarios without inventing chapter titles.

The boy lives in Sakuya with Haruka, his practical, protective mother, and Kaname, a game producer whose childhood enthusiasm for Nazkaiser still shapes his ideas of heroism. His friends are outspoken Ganta and supermarket-owner's son Kiichi. Transfer student Nanami Yoshida has moved from Kiriya with her mother, Takako. Her father, Takashi Sakura, remains in Kiriya following the parents' divorce.

**Opening: "The Beginning Day" and the first rescue (2495-2503).** Ganta and Kiichi give the boy Nanami's lost Nazkaiser handkerchief. An errand for Haruka leads him to Pork Shrine, Nanami's dog, and a strange man. The shrine's cherry tree is flowering out of season and exhausting its life force. The man seeks Nanami's tears. When yokai abduct her, the boy rescues her from Ginger Pork and earns her recognition as a hero. Kaname's familiarity with Nanami and his gift of Kiriya sweets unsettle Haruka.

**Holiday promises and fireworks (2505-2511).** Nanami recruits the boy to investigate the connection between the shrine tree and Kiriya's failing tree. His commitments to her clash with promises to his friends and mother. The family cat rescues him from Kitenso. The strange man is Kikichi, an ancestor of Nanami; an old pact involving Otane, a pig, and a cherry cutting emerges piecemeal. Nanami's apartment tower blocks energy traveling between the trees, scattering it and strengthening yokai. Ganta feels abandoned and breaks off their friendship.

**Reconciliation and the haunted school (2515-2517, with dungeon dialogue in 2512-2513).** Nanami takes responsibility for the broken promises. Ganta demands proof of yokai, but Garanryu separates the children at school. The boy recovers his friends' belongings, solves puzzles, and survives a counterfeit Nanami. Shared danger restores the boys' loyalty. School records and Nanami's discomfort with "Yoshida" expose the family's rupture.

**Journey and factory investigation (2518-2525).** Ganta, Kiichi, and the dog help the children evade the adults and board a train. In Kiriya, Nanami explains that Takashi supported a worker-funded factory, contributing to the divorce. Croakmire admits poisoning the ground to harm the trees and implicate the Sakura family. His defeat is insufficient: the tree was already declining, and the pact still needs renewal. Kojima, Nanami's childhood would-be sweetheart, becomes vulnerable to Waverwood after rejection. Takashi falls protecting his family and disappears into another time. Tears of Sorrow fail as the offering.

**The animals' parallel investigations (2528-2603).** The cat uncovers the trees' parent-child relationship and recruits Squawks and Dr. Joshu to communicate with humans. It remembers Haruka adopting it ten years earlier. Kaname admits helping Takako, his childhood friend and former girlfriend; his evasiveness intensifies Haruka's fears. The dog witnesses Nanami's loneliness, Takashi's promise to stay away, and Takako's unresolved attachment to Kaname. Its polite appeals repeatedly sound like ordinary barking to humans.

**Death, the pact, and the ninth life (2559-2566; overlapping 2596-2599).** A Waverwood-influenced traffic incident kills Kaname and the cat. The Bird Shogunate judges the dead. Kikichi's forbidden pact bought the tree 200 years of health using tears from the person he loved most, his daughter Otane. Coojudge lets Kaname return at a chosen moment in exchange for feeding pigeons, but excludes the cat. Takashi later learns the missing condition: the tears must express joy. The cat gives its ninth and final life to empower the boy.

**Finale and aftermath (2526-2527, 2600-2606).** The boy transforms into Nazkaiser; the dog also fights for Nanami. Waverwood possesses Takako, mixing its hostility with her resentment toward Kaname and Haruka. Takashi retrieves Nanami's joyful tears from the dog's birth and forces Coojudge to return him. The trees recover. Nanami's family considers living together again; reconciliation is not guaranteed. The boy's family mourns the cat. A year later, blossoms return and the narrator reveals himself as the boy. Twelve years later, Kaname writes successful yokai books but cannot recall the crucial events. Takako is present in his domestic life and Haruka is elsewhere; the precise legal arrangement remains unstated.
